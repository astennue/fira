How much are FIRA's placement fees?Category: Fees

FIRA charges no placement fee for domestic helper and caregiver categories, as fees are employer-paid per DMW rules. For skills professionals, the maximum allowable fee is one month's salary, collected only after contract signing — never before deployment.

How long does deployment to Morocco take?Category: Deployment

The average timeline from application to departure is 3–5 months: 2–4 weeks for matching and interviews, 4–8 weeks for employer contract and work permit, and 6–8 weeks for medical, OEC, and visa processing. Your FIRA officer provides weekly status updates through the platform.

What documents do I need to register?Category: Application

At minimum: a valid passport (6+ months), PSA birth certificate, NBI clearance, and TIN. Skills professionals additionally need PRC licenses and certificates. The platform's resume parser auto-fills your profile from your uploaded documents.

Is FIRA a licensed agency?Category: Legal

Yes. FIRA (Fil International Recruitment Agency) operates under DMW License No. POEA-218-LB-101521-R with a Casablanca branch registered under Moroccan labor law. Verify our license anytime at dmw.gov.ph.
